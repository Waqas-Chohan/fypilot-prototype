About FYPilot

Built to end a 40-hour paper chase

Every semester, FYP coordinators spend 40+ hours manually transcribing rubrics, chasing feedback, and compiling accreditation data. Students wait days for results and have no way to verify their proposal is original.

FYPilot solves this with a mobile-first portal: AI-powered OCR turns handwritten rubrics into Flex Portal-ready grades, a pgvector similarity engine checks originality against the archive, and every stakeholder — from student to library staff — gets a dashboard built for exactly what they need.
